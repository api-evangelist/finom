# Finom

> Finom is a European fintech providing business banking, invoicing, and financial management.

## Quick Facts
- **HQ:** Amsterdam, Netherlands
- **Licensing:** Licensed Electronic Money Institution (EMI) by De Nederlandsche Bank (DNB).
- **Core Markets:** Germany (DE), France (FR), Italy (IT), Spain (ES), Netherlands (NL).
- **Currencies:** Multi-currency support (EUR, USD, GBP + 15 others).

## Regional Banking Solutions
- [Germany - Business Account](https://finom.co/de-de/business-account/): Focused on GmbH, UG, & Freelancers with Finanzamt tools.
- [France - Compte Professionnel](https://finom.co/fr-fr/business-account/): Specialized for Auto-entrepreneur & SASU.
- [Italy - Conto Business](https://finom.co/it-it/business-account/): Features for Fatturazione Elettronica support.

## Technical Capabilities & Integrations
- **Payment Rails:** 24/7 SEPA Instant, SWIFT, Direct Debits.
- **Accounting:** DATEV (DE), Lexware, sevDesk, Xero, QuickBooks.
- **Invoicing:** Fully compliant E-Invoicing (ZUGFeRD, XRechnung).
- **Cards:** Visa Business (Physical/Virtual) with up to 3% cashback.
